---
description: The part of a website that runs in your browser: everything you see and click.
---

The frontend is the [client](./Client.md) side of a [website](./Website.md) — the layout, buttons, menus, animations and [forms](./Form.md). It's built from [HTML](./HTML.md), [CSS](./CSS.md) and [JavaScript](./JavaScript.md), often with the help of a [framework](./Framework.md).

Frontend developers care about how things look and feel: [responsive design](./Responsive%20design.md), [accessibility](./Accessibility.md), smooth interactions and [web performance](./Web%20performance.md). The frontend gets its data by calling the [backend](./Backend.md), usually through an [API](./API.md).

Because frontend code runs on the visitor's device, anyone can see and change it. Secrets and important rules belong on the backend.

_Avoid:_ "frontend" as just the visual design — it's working code, with its own logic, testing and performance concerns.

_Usage:_

"Can the frontend team add a discount for VIP customers?"

"They can show the badge, but the backend has to decide who's VIP and apply the price."
