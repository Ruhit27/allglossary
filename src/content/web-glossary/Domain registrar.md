---
description: A company that sells and manages the registration of domain names.
---

You can't buy a [domain name](./Domain%20name.md) directly from the organizations that run .com or .org — you go through a registrar, such as Namecheap, Cloudflare or GoDaddy. The registrar records you as the domain's owner, usually for one year at a time, and lets you renew it.

Registrars also let you manage the domain's [DNS](./Domain%20Name%20System%20%28DNS%29.md) records, or point them to another provider — often your [hosting](./Hosting.md) company. That's how a domain gets connected to a [website](./Website.md).

Keep the registrar account secure and renewals automatic. A lapsed domain can be snapped up by someone else within days.

_Avoid:_ "registrar" and "host" as the same job — the registrar manages the name; hosting runs the site, even when one company sells both.

_Usage:_

"Can we move the domain to a cheaper registrar?"

"Yes. Unlock it and transfer it; the site stays up as long as the DNS records come along."
