---
description: A company that connects homes and businesses to the internet.
---

The company you pay for your internet connection: a broadband provider, a cable company, or a mobile network. Your ISP links your home network to the rest of the [internet](./Internet.md) and gives it a public [IP address](./IP%20address.md).

By default, your ISP also handles your [DNS](./Domain%20Name%20System%20%28DNS%29.md) lookups, turning [domain names](./Domain%20name.md) into addresses. Because your traffic passes through it, an ISP can see which sites you connect to — though [HTTPS](./HTTPS.md) hides what you do on those sites.

If one website is slow, the problem is usually that site. If every site is slow, the problem is more likely your Wi-Fi or your ISP.

_Avoid:_ blaming the ISP for one broken website — if other sites load fine, the connection is working.

_Usage:_

"Our site is down for everyone at home."

"Try it on mobile data. If it loads there, it's our ISP, not the site."
