---
description: The pop-up asking visitors whether a site may use cookies and tracking.
---

The "Accept all / Reject / Settings" box you see on many [websites](./Website.md). Privacy laws, especially in Europe, say sites must ask before storing non-essential [cookies](./Cookie.md) or [tracking](./Tracking.md) visitors. The banner is how they ask.

Essential cookies — keeping you [logged in](./Login.md), remembering your cart — don't need consent. Analytics and advertising cookies, including [third-party cookies](./Third-party%20cookie.md), usually do. A proper banner must make rejecting as easy as accepting, and the site must actually wait for the answer before loading the trackers.

Many banners fail that test, using tricks like hiding the reject button. Regulators have started fining companies for it.

_Avoid:_ "accepting cookies" as one yes-or-no choice — a proper banner lets you allow some uses, like analytics, and refuse others, like advertising.

_Usage:_

"Can we make the Reject button grey and small?"

"No, it has to be as easy as Accept. Regulators fine sites for that."
