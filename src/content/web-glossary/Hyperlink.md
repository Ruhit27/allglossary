The connection that makes the [World Wide Web](./World%20Wide%20Web.md) a web. A hyperlink (usually just "link") points to a [URL](./URL.md); click it and your [browser](./Browser.md) goes there. In [HTML](./HTML.md) it's the `<a>` tag.

Links can go to another [website](./Website.md), another [page](./Web%20page.md) on the same site, a specific spot on the current page, or a file to download. Clear link text — "read our refund policy" rather than "click here" — helps everyone, especially people using screen readers, and helps [search engines](./Search%20engine%20optimization%20%28SEO%29.md) understand what the linked page is about.

Links also rely on stable URLs. When a page moves without a redirect, every link to it breaks: a "dead link".

_Avoid:_ "click here" as link text — say where the link goes.

_Usage:_

"Customers can't find the returns page."

"Add a link to it in the footer and on every order confirmation."
