---
description: Renting space on a server, so a website is stored and available online.
---

For people to visit your [website](./Website.md), its files and code have to live on a [server](./Server.md) that's always on and connected to the [internet](./Internet.md). Hosting companies provide that. Options range from cheap shared hosting, through platforms built for [static sites](./Static%20site.md) and [frameworks](./Framework.md), to renting whole machines in the [cloud](./Cloud.md).

Modern hosts often bundle [HTTPS](./HTTPS.md) certificates, a [CDN](./Content%20delivery%20network%20%28CDN%29.md) and automatic [deploys](./Deploy.md) from [version control](./Version%20control.md). You connect your [domain name](./Domain%20name.md) to the host through [DNS](./Domain%20Name%20System%20%28DNS%29.md).

Hosting and domains are separate purchases. You can keep your domain and move to a new host just by changing DNS.

_Avoid:_ confusing hosting with your domain — the domain is the name; hosting is where the site actually runs.

_Usage:_

"Do we need to change our domain to switch hosts?"

"No. Keep the domain and point its DNS at the new host."
