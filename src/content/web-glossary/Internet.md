---
description: The global network of networks that lets computers anywhere exchange data.
---

A worldwide network made by connecting millions of smaller networks — home Wi-Fi, office networks, mobile networks, data centers. Any device on it can send data to any other, using shared rules for addressing and delivery. Every device gets an [IP address](./IP%20address.md), and you usually reach the internet through an [internet service provider](./Internet%20service%20provider%20%28ISP%29.md).

The internet is the plumbing. Many services run on top of it: email, video calls, online games, and the most familiar one, the [World Wide Web](./World%20Wide%20Web.md). When you open a [website](./Website.md), your [browser](./Browser.md) uses the internet to reach a [server](./Server.md) that may be on the other side of the planet.

No single company owns or runs the internet. It works because everyone agrees to follow the same open standards.

_Avoid:_ "internet" and "web" as the same thing — the web is one service that runs on the internet.

_Usage:_

"The internet is down, I can't load any websites."

"Your messaging app still works, so the internet is fine. It's probably just the Wi-Fi's DNS setting."
