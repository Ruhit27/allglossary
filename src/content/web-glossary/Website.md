---
description: A group of related web pages published together under one domain name.
---

A set of [web pages](./Web%20page.md) that belong together — a company's pages, a blog, an online shop — usually living under one [domain name](./Domain%20name.md) like example.com. The pages link to each other and share a look, a menu and an owner.

A website is stored on a [server](./Server.md) and delivered to visitors' [browsers](./Browser.md) over the [internet](./Internet.md). Some websites are a handful of fixed pages (a [static site](./Static%20site.md)); others build pages on demand and behave like full applications, with logins, shopping carts and dashboards.

To have a website you need three things: the files that make up the pages, [hosting](./Hosting.md) to serve them, and a domain name pointing to that hosting.

_Avoid:_ "website" and "web page" interchangeably — a website is the whole collection; a page is one document in it.

_Usage:_

"Can you send me the website?"

"Do you want the whole site's address, or the link to that one pricing page?"
