---
description: Building pages so they adapt to any screen size, from phone to wide monitor.
---

Making one [web page](./Web%20page.md) that rearranges itself to fit whatever screen it's on. On a wide monitor, content might sit in three columns; on a phone, the same content stacks into one, with a menu that folds behind a button.

It works mostly through [CSS](./CSS.md): layouts that stretch, images that resize, and rules that switch in at certain widths ("breakpoints"). Most web traffic now comes from phones, so many teams design the phone layout first and expand it for bigger screens.

Responsive design goes hand in hand with [accessibility](./Accessibility.md) and [web performance](./Web%20performance.md): a phone user on a slow connection shouldn't have to download a desktop-sized image.

_Avoid:_ "responsive" meaning fast — here it means the layout adapts to screen size, not how quickly the page reacts.

_Usage:_

"The pricing table is cut off on my phone."

"It's not responsive yet. Let's stack the plans vertically on small screens."
