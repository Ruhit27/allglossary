description: A system of linked pages and resources that you open in a browser, running on top of the internet.
---

The web is the collection of [web pages](./Web%20page.md) and other resources, connected by [hyperlinks](./Hyperlink.md), that you browse with a [browser](./Browser.md). Each resource has its own address, a [URL](./URL.md), and is fetched using [HTTP](./HTTP.md). It was invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989 to help scientists share documents.

The web runs on the [internet](./Internet.md) but is only one part of it. What makes it "the web" is the linking: any page can point to any other page, anywhere, without asking permission. Those links turn billions of separate [websites](./Website.md) into one connected space.

Today the web carries far more than documents — shops, maps, video, full applications — but it is still built on the same three ideas: URLs to name things, HTTP to fetch them, and [HTML](./HTML.md) to describe them.

_Avoid:_ "the web" for every online service — email and many phone apps use the internet without being part of the web.

_Usage:_

"Is our app on the web?"

"It's a phone app that talks to our API. It isn't on the web until there's a site people can open in a browser."
