---
description: Improving a website so search engines understand it and show it higher in results.
---

Search engines like Google send programs ("crawlers") to read [web pages](./Web%20page.md), follow [hyperlinks](./Hyperlink.md), and decide which pages best answer each search. SEO is helping them: clear page titles and descriptions, meaningful headings in the [HTML](./HTML.md), descriptive links, and content that actually answers what people search for.

The technical side matters too. Pages should load fast ([web performance](./Web%20performance.md)), work on phones ([responsive design](./Responsive%20design.md)), use [HTTPS](./HTTPS.md), return the right [status code](./Status%20code.md), and show their content in the HTML — [server-side rendering](./Server-side%20rendering.md) or a [static site](./Static%20site.md) helps.

Links from other trusted sites are a strong signal. Tricks to fool search engines tend to get caught and penalized.

_Avoid:_ "SEO" as paying for search ads — ads are separate; SEO is about earning the unpaid places in search results.

_Usage:_

"Why don't our product pages show up on Google?"

"The crawler sees empty pages because they're rendered in the browser. Let's render them on the server."
